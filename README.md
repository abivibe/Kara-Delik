# 🇬🇧 English Guide

## 🕳️ What is Kara Delik (Black Hole)?
Tired of dealing with ad-ridden, pop-up infested, slow, and sketchy websites just to save a video or audio track from the web?

**Kara Delik (Black Hole)** is a sleek, **AMOLED Black**, ad-free, and blazingly fast desktop media downloader built to end that frustration. Just like a real black hole: **Drop the link in, and it pulls the media straight to your PC in seconds!**

### 🌟 Key Features
- **🎬 All Major Platforms in One Place:**
  - **YouTube:** 4K, 2K, 1080p HD, 720p videos, YouTube Shorts, and full Playlists.
  - **Instagram:** Reels, posts, and videos in original quality.
  - **TikTok:** Download videos **completely watermark-free** in crisp HD.
  - **X (Twitter) & Facebook:** Direct MP4 video extraction with zero hassle.
- **🎧 Studio-Grade Audio (MP3):** Convert music videos, lectures, or podcasts directly into **320kbps MP3** audio with a single click.
- **🚀 Turbo Multi-Channel Engine:** Powered by an integrated concurrent range-request engine that splits streams into parallel chunks, bypassing bandwidth throttling and maximizing your connection speed.
- **📋 Smart Clipboard Listener:** The moment you copy a supported video link anywhere on your PC, Kara Delik detects it and offers instant one-click downloading via a native notification.
- **📊 Instant File Size Resolution:** Calculates and displays the exact file size before you even hit download.
- **🖤 Pure AMOLED Minimalism:** Zero bloatware, zero ads, zero clutter. Just pure performance wrapped in a modern dark UI.

### 📥 Installation (How to Download & Install)
Getting started takes less than **30 seconds**:

1. Head over to the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** section on the right side of this repository.
2. Under **"Kara Delik v1.0.0 (Installer Package)"**, download **`KaraDelik_Setup.exe`**.
3. Run `KaraDelik_Setup.exe` and follow the quick setup wizard.
4. Launch **Kara Delik** from your Desktop shortcut and start downloading! *(All required media processing engines are bundled inside the installer—no external dependencies needed).*
